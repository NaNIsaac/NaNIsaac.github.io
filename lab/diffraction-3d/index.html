What is actually calculated?

The crystal contains 9 × 7 × 7 identical point scatterers on a simple-cubic lattice. For each detector direction, the program sums their scattered complex amplitudes and squares the magnitude. For this regular lattice, the sum becomes a product of three finite-lattice interference factors.

I(q) ∝ |Σj exp(iq · rj)|² ·   q = kout − kin

The first-order Bragg angle obeys 2d sin θ = λ. This is a kinematic, single-scattering demonstration. It does not model multiple scattering, dynamical diffraction, absorption, strain, detector resolution, or photon noise. No experimental dataset is reproduced here.
