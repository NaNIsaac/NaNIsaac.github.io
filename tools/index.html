The lab is for playing with ideas; this page is for getting an answer during beamtime. Every field recalculates as you type. Energies in keV, lengths in Å unless a field says otherwise. The full offline set, with a beamtime logbook, lives at xray.ooguy.com.

Energy ↔ wavelengthBragg angleq and d-spacingBeam footprintTransmission

Photon energy and wavelength

λ = hc / E, with hc = 12.39842 keV·Å.

Bragg angle

λ = 2d sin θ. The defaults are Si(111) with Cu Kα1, 2θ = 28.44°.

Momentum transfer q and d-spacing

q = 4π sin θ / λ and d = 2π / q. For reflectivity, θ is the grazing angle and q = qz.

Beam footprint

L = h / sin α. When L is longer than the sample, the sample stops seeing the whole beam and the measured intensity drops.

Transmission

T = exp(−t / ℓ). Look the attenuation length up for your material and energy, for example in the CXRO tables.
